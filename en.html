Privacy Policy

Nexora Ledger. Last updated: October 4, 2026.

Data we collect

  • Email, name, phone number, and user ID.
  • Financial records the user enters: income, expenses, purchases, checks, debts, advances, month close, and notes.
  • Check photos the user attaches.
  • This data is stored on Supabase servers.

Why do we use the data?

  • We use the data only to provide the service: running the store ledger, showing records to people with permission, and backup and restore.
  • No ads, no tracking, and no selling of data.

Account deletion

  • You can delete your account in the app from My stores → Delete account.
  • You must re-enter your password. If the account owns active stores, those stores are archived first or their ownership is transferred, and their financial history is kept.
  • Deletion removes the sign-in account, sessions, email, phone, and name. Financial records, check photos, and the audit log stay linked to an anonymous ID so the ledger history does not change.
